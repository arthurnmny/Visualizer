About

A small practice, focused on research data.

Tofutopia is run by one person and works on one kind of problem: getting a research dataset into a state where it can be analyzed.

Most research groups don't have — and don't need — a full-time data team. What they need is someone who can take a messy dataset and return it in a usable state, with the decisions documented along the way.

That's the work Tofutopia does. It's run by Arthur Yang, who brings over 10 years of experience in data analysis, engineering, and infrastructure at a FAANG company to research work that has no data team behind it.
